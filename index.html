Flood maps for Britain: England, Wales and Scotland

Floodplain Atlas brings separate national datasets into one interactive map. England and Wales show Flood Zones 2 and 3; Scotland shows SEPA river and coastal scenarios; the Republic of Ireland shows OPW current-scenario extents. Northern Ireland is not yet covered.

Classifications differ between sources. This is a broad exploration map, not an official planning service, live warning map or property-level assessment.

Official sources: Environment Agency, Natural Resources Wales, SEPA, and the OPW flood data catalogue.
