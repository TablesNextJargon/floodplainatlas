About the map and data

Floodplain Atlas is an interactive map of official river, sea and coastal flood extent data for England, Wales, Scotland and the Republic of Ireland. It combines separate national datasets: Environment Agency Flood Zones 2 and 3, Natural Resources Wales planning zones, SEPA v3.0 river and coastal scenarios, and OPW river and coastal scenarios. Classifications differ by country; the map shows simplified extents, not flood depth, live warnings or property-level risk. It excludes Northern Ireland and Scottish surface-water mapping.
